Privacy Policy

Course Tracker · by The Altitude Mindset

Course Tracker does not collect, transmit, or store any of your information. There is no account, no server, and no analytics.

In short: everything you log stays in your browser on your own device. Nobody — including us — can see it.

What the app stores

Your habits, your daily ticks, and your flight days are saved in your browser's localStorage on the device you are using. This data never leaves the device. It is not uploaded, backed up to us, or shared with any third party.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no account system, no login, no telemetry, no crash reporting, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs. It makes no network requests after the initial page load, which is why it works fully offline.

Export and import

You can export your history to a .json file and import it on another device. That file is created by your device and saved wherever you choose. It is never sent anywhere by the app; moving it between devices is entirely under your control.

Deleting your data

Use Reset month to clear a single month, delete individual habits from the habits panel, or clear the site data / uninstall the app to remove everything. Because we hold no copy, uninstalling erases it permanently — export first if you want to keep it.

Children

The app is a general-purpose habit tracker and is not directed at children. It collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.

Hosting

The app is served as static files from GitHub Pages. As with any website, GitHub's servers may log standard request information such as IP address when the page is downloaded. That logging is GitHub's, is outside our control, and is described in GitHub's Privacy Statement. Once loaded, the app makes no further requests.
